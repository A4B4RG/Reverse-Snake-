# Deep canopy visual direction

The canopy theme has two palettes. Light is the default: pale ivory and mint surfaces, deep teal text, darker amber accents and green foliage. Dark preserves the original dark teal jungle with golden accents. Both retain the golden mango, amber snake with diamond markings, mossy stones and turquoise/teal close-call leaves. Light artwork adds dark outlines around the fruit and snake head for separation from the pale ground. Character silhouettes remain different from hazards; foliage stays outside playable cells.

Menu → Theme exposes labeled Light/Dark selections. The chosen palette applies to the entire page, dialogs, SVG maps and Canvas across Mango, Hunter, Challenges and Replays. A separate browser-local preference restores before initial paint and synchronizes between tabs; unsupported or unavailable storage defaults to light. Theme switching redraws a paused board without advancing its rules or resetting characters. Menu toggles on click/tap or keyboard activation, highlights hovered/focused options, and scrolls internally on short screens. Hovering over the trigger leaves gameplay running. Mango offers the Hunter mode link; Hunter offers the Mango mode link. Opening Menu pauses the chase or replay until explicitly resumed.

Hunter reuses the same board artwork and amber snake; the mango is a collectible. Local Challenge retains the mango chase. Ghosts are faint turquoise outlines drawn before live characters, so they cannot obscure collision targets. Replay uses the same renderer as the recorded mode.

The page now allocates available viewport height to the board. Promotional copy is reduced where space is limited; core setup and controls remain reachable. Main controls are at least 44 px. At short board heights, overlays retain titles and primary actions and omit secondary decorative copy. Phone controls use a compact horizontal direction row. Help remains available through the menu or mode guide.

Mango's board has no tracking badge, vertical side slogans or bottom status/arena footer. Mode and difficulty sections show their controls without supporting captions. The dash meter reports readiness concisely. The mango sticker uses 10 px text, a bounded text width and a small upward offset to keep the phrase inside the compact oval.

Reduced motion keeps the aura and boost countdown while suppressing moving trails and bursts. No new raster artwork, external font request or downloaded sound was introduced.
