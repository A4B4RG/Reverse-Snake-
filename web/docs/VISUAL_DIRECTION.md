# Canopy presentation

Light remains the default pale ivory/mint canopy with deep teal ink and amber accents. Dark retains the original teal surfaces and bright accents. Menu → Theme changes all routes, dialogs, maps and Canvas. Selection persists locally and synchronizes between tabs. Menu opens by click, tap or keyboard; hover highlights options. Opening it pauses gameplay/replay until resumed.

The game identity is fruit-neutral. Apple/Jade are the fresh defaults, with Mango, Pear, Orange and Strawberry skins and Amber, Jade, Coral and Midnight snakes available through named Food character / Snake design dropdowns in Menu. Choose once; saved choices carry into later matches. Each fruit has full artwork, and snakes use diamonds, bands, spots or chevrons with distinct colors. The hero ornament uses the selected fruit without a promotional caption. Skin selection persists and is disabled during active/paused rounds.

Canvas fills the entire outer board rectangle. Flexible columns/rows keep cell proportions close to square across wide, tall and fullscreen layouts; fullscreen adds actual playable cells. Thirty maps use root corners, lanes, islands, courtyards and maze walls. Walls scale by full footprint so fullscreen retains occupied area. A map/score progress strip sits above the board, with an unlock marker and three stars at target +100, +300 and +701. Earned stars use gold fill. Next map becomes enabled without changing the row height. Hunter shares that strip in Levels; lightning/snowflake collectibles and active-effect countdown chips identify its power-ups. The main board keeps score/time, pause/fullscreen and boost feedback. Previously removed tracking badges, side slogans and footer/setup captions remain absent. “A few handy moves” is a compact two-column panel directly below the desktop game frame, matching its width; setup remains in the sidebar. The main hero uses a 26–34 px heading, a smaller fruit ornament and less padding, with a 24 px heading on compact portrait layouts. Compact screens hide the hero and setup sidebar, putting mode/pace/map/course choices in Menu. The board gets most of the viewport. Directional swipes and double-tap dash replace the arrow pad and Dash button; a brief ready-state hint explains them.

Matching ghosts use their recorded full fruit artwork composited once at 32% opacity. The pause overlay is translucent and applies a 6 px backdrop blur; the user's last recorded fruit-and-snake match loops behind it through a separate cursor. Live gameplay stays frozen. With no earlier history, the current prefix is used. Reduced-motion preference suppresses that playback and moving boost effects while retaining state feedback.

All artwork remains code-native; no raster asset generator, external font fetch or downloaded sound is required. Asset provenance and third-party notices remain in ASSET_MANIFEST.md and ASSET_LICENCES.md.

The welcome flow shows the game logo and Start, two role cards, then mode-specific instructions. Hover/focus wraps Hunter with an SVG snake; Food fades as an apple emerges. Small/short screens show one of four instruction tips at a time. The Start arrow floats while its button remains steady. Golden halos and revolving stars show stuns; inverse-color electric zigzags follow the boosted Hunter body. Bite jaws/crumbs appear on all captures. Hard Hunter walls sink and rise with water ripples as terrain changes. Reduced motion keeps static status cues. Current welcome/tip and Hunter effect captures are in screenshots/welcome-*.png and screenshots/hunter-*-animation.png.
