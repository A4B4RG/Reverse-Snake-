# Canopy presentation

Light remains the default pale ivory/mint canopy with deep teal ink and amber accents. Dark retains the original teal surfaces and bright accents. Menu → Theme changes all routes, dialogs, maps and Canvas. Selection persists locally and synchronizes between tabs. Menu opens by click, tap or keyboard; hover highlights options. Opening it pauses gameplay/replay until resumed.

The game identity is fruit-neutral. Apple/Jade are the fresh defaults, with Mango, Pear, Orange and Strawberry skins and Amber, Jade, Coral and Midnight snakes available before every match. Each fruit has full artwork, and snakes use diamonds, bands, spots or chevrons with distinct colors. The hero ornament uses the selected fruit without a promotional caption. Skin selection persists and is disabled during active/paused rounds.

Canvas fills the entire outer board rectangle. Flexible columns/rows keep cell proportions close to square across wide, tall and fullscreen layouts; fullscreen adds actual playable cells. Terrain fills that geometry rather than sitting inside a centered fixed-size square. The main board keeps score/time, pause/fullscreen and boost feedback. Previously removed tracking badges, side slogans and footer/setup captions remain absent. “A few handy moves” sits at the sidebar bottom on desktop; compact screen layouts retain setup and touch controls.

Matching ghosts use their recorded full fruit artwork composited once at 32% opacity. The pause overlay is translucent and applies a 6 px backdrop blur; the user's last recorded fruit-and-snake match loops behind it through a separate cursor. Live gameplay stays frozen. With no earlier history, the current prefix is used. Reduced-motion preference suppresses that playback and moving boost effects while retaining state feedback.

Current captures are in `screenshots/adaptive-*.png` and `screenshots/theme-*.png`, including desktop/phone, both palettes, pause and fullscreen. They are observations of particular local production builds, not a physical-device or full accessibility certification. All artwork remains code-native; no raster asset generator, external font fetch or downloaded sound is required. Asset provenance and third-party notices remain in ASSET_MANIFEST.md and ASSET_LICENCES.md.
