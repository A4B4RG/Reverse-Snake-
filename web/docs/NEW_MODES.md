# Mode and recording contracts

Escape Levels and Survival use the same flexible board geometry as Hunter and Challenges. Successful fruit moves train the local fruit policy; successful player-controlled snake moves train the local snake policy. Legal collisions, score multipliers, close calls and the manual dash retain their rules. Opponents combine learned predictions and movement preferences with legal routing. The learning state is shared between modes and saved separately from scores.

Hunter starts a four-cell player-controlled snake facing right. It moves at the selected difficulty cadence, buffers two perpendicular turns and rejects reversals. The fruit moves to evade the snake at 520/340/220 ms on Easy/Normal/Hard. Human snake motion teaches the snake network; the fruit uses that prediction and its learned movement policy to choose legal exits. Eating fruit adds one cell and 100 points times the difficulty multiplier. Walls, stones and body collisions lose. The departing tail is traversable unless growing; filling all free cells wins. Seeded spawning enumerates free cells rather than retrying random positions. Dash and close-call boosts belong to fruit-controlled modes.

Canopy Sprint, Root Runner and Wild Escape keep their fixed course IDs, arenas, seeds and 15/20/25-second objectives. Their board dimensions follow the available viewport and their opponents use the player's current learned models. Capture takes precedence over reaching the target. They are personal practice courses; a fixed seed does not promise identical adaptive opponents across attempts or devices. Results are stored separately by challenge ID and rules version.

Fruit and snake appearance can be selected before every match and is frozen during the match. Gameplay hitboxes remain cell-based for every skin. Scores remain browser-local practice records; there is no online leaderboard or cross-board competitive guarantee.

Replay format 2, version 1.2.0, ruleset `orchard-3`, captures the initial geometry, appearance and neural snapshots plus ordered steer, clear, dash, pause, resume, resize and fixed-step actions. Training uses the recorded inputs, so evolving weights and final fingerprints reproduce deterministically. Consecutive identical steps are compressed. The retained legacy engines support format 1 / version 1.1.0 / `canopy-2` recordings without silently applying new rules. Unknown versions, malformed weights/dimensions/actions and oversized recordings are rejected. Three completed recordings are retained, each bounded to 250 KB, 20,000 action groups and 180,000 steps. Wall time, audio, render cadence and generated score dates/IDs are outside playback guarantees.

Matching Escape/Survival ghosts use their recorded full fruit skin at 32% opacity and require the same mode, arena, difficulty, seed and dimensions. Cosmetics or models may differ because the ghost represents its own recorded round. Live resize stops the incompatible ghost. It never changes collisions, scores or training.

Paused gameplay uses an independent replay cursor looping the last saved gameplay, with the current recorded prefix as a first-visit fallback. The blurred translucent overlay covers that presentation while the live round and its normal ghost timeline remain frozen. The separate bounded preview key may hold an unscored/abandoned prefix; it does not create a completed score or a replay-history entry. Reduced motion keeps the preview static.
