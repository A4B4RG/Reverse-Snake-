# Mode and recording contracts

Escape Levels, Survival, Hunter and Challenges share flexible board geometry and local learning. Fruit moves teach the fruit policy; player-controlled snake moves teach the snake policy. Opponents combine learned predictions with legal routing.

Levels uses thirty increasing score gates: the first ten range from 150 to 1300, and maps 11–30 rise from 1500 to 5300 in steps of 200. Each successful moved cell earns 1 / 1.5 / 2 points on Easy / Normal / Hard. Idle and blocked movement earns none. Reaching a target unlocks the next map without stopping the chase. Qualification is saved live; same-step capture takes precedence. Next map voluntarily finishes/records the current run before configuring the following map. End chase in pause records a deliberate ending. Food hunters grow every 20 active seconds on Easy and 10 on Normal/Hard. Close dodges trim one tail cell, collisions trim two and stun in place for 1.6 seconds. The tail then leads the chase. After 60 seconds a body of at least 18 cells splits once into two. Snakes cannot overlap; a simultaneous collision stuns both complete bodies and both tails become heads afterward. Splitting applies to Food modes only. Unlocks persist separately from score history in the existing save; older stored wins migrate. Clearing score history keeps unlocks and stars. Star milestones are target +100, +300 and strictly above +700; best ratings persist even after capture. Survival offers every map, scores 10 points/second times difficulty, uses the same growth, split and in-place collision rules.

Fresh Food/Hunter sessions start on Easy; existing saved Food difficulty remains respected. Hunter starts a four-cell player-controlled snake facing right. It moves at the selected difficulty cadence, buffers two perpendicular turns and rejects reversals. Fruit evades at 400 / 133⅓ / 80 ms on Easy/Normal/Hard, against snake steps of 200 / 100 / 80 ms using learned choices and predicted human snake motion. Eating fruit adds one cell and 100 points times difficulty. Walls, stones and body collisions lose. The departing tail is traversable unless growing; filling all free cells wins. Seeded spawning enumerates free cells. Hunter Levels uses the same map targets and star milestones with separate saved progress; qualification does not end the hunt. Hunter Survival offers all maps. Hunter has no fruit dash or close-call boost. Lightning pickups double snake speed for 10 seconds; snowflakes freeze food for 5 seconds. Two items start on clear cells, collected items replenish after 8 active seconds, and their timers pause/resize without resetting. Starts randomize the seed so item positions vary between rounds. Hunter also grows with active time. Hard walls start shifting after 45 seconds, repeat every 30, sink for 600 ms and rise in their new places for 600 ms. Activation excludes current actors/items. Hunter never splits.

Canopy Sprint, Root Runner and Wild Escape retain fixed course IDs, arenas, seeds and 15/20/25-second objectives. Their board dimensions follow the viewport and opponents use current learned models. Capture takes precedence over the target. They are personal practice courses; a fixed seed does not promise identical adaptive opponents across devices. Results are stored separately by challenge ID and rules version.

One named Food character / Snake design option pair lives in Menu. Choices persist across rounds/routes/reloads until changed and freeze during active/paused play. Gameplay hitboxes remain cell-based. Touch swipes up/down/left/right set their matching direction; taps do not steer. Double-tap after a swipe dashes along the last direction in fruit-controlled modes. The three-cell dash, four-second cooldown and close-call boosts retain their collision/timing rules. Pause/restart clears continuing swipe movement. Fullscreen transitions retain input and restore canvas focus; actual hidden/blur suspension still pauses. Food base step intervals are 100 / 100 / 120 ms against hunter steps of 200 / 100 / 80 ms. Keyboard controls remain available; on-screen arrows and Dash buttons are removed.

Replay format 2, version 1.5.0, ruleset `jungle-6`, captures geometry, appearance, initial neural snapshots and ordered steer, clear, dash, pause, resume, resize, voluntary finish and fixed-step actions. Hunter metadata includes Levels/Survival, and seeded power-up placement, growth, stuns, splits, bites and wall relocation are simulated during playback. Deterministic training reproduces evolving weights and final fingerprints. Consecutive identical steps are compressed. Retained engines support 1.4.0 / `orchard-5` / format 2, 1.3.0 / `orchard-4` / format 2, 1.2.0 / `orchard-3` / format 2 and 1.1.0 / `canopy-2` / format 1 with original terrain/rules. Unknown versions, malformed weights/dimensions/actions and oversized recordings are rejected. Three completed recordings are retained, each bounded to 250 KB, 20,000 action groups and 180,000 steps. Wall time, audio, render cadence and generated IDs/dates are outside playback guarantees.

Matching fruit-controlled ghosts use recorded full artwork at 32% opacity and require the same mode, map, difficulty, seed and dimensions. Live resize stops incompatible ghosts. Cosmetics/models can differ because the ghost represents its own recorded run; it never changes collisions, scoring or training.

Pause loops an independent cursor through saved gameplay, using the current prefix as a fallback, behind a blurred translucent overlay. The live round and regular ghost stay frozen. The bounded preview key may hold unscored/abandoned prefixes without creating score or completed-replay entries. Reduced motion keeps the preview static. Scores remain browser-local; cross-ruleset records are not a competitive comparison. Online PvP is described separately in ONLINE_PVP.md and is not implemented.
