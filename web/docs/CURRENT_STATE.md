# Current browser product

Updated 14 November 2026 for version 1.2.0, ruleset `orchard-3`. This is the current product and repository scenario. The root `PROJECT_STATUS.md` remains a historical progress snapshot and is not a completion specification.

Reverse Snake is a fruit-neutral browser game in `web/`, built with Next.js 16.4.5, React 19.3.1, TypeScript, Node 24 and Canvas. Levels and Survival offer five arenas and Easy, Normal and Hard. Hunter lets the player control the snake against an evasive fruit. Three timed local Challenges and completed-run Replays are available on separate routes. Light is the default theme; the original dark palette remains selectable in the click/tap/keyboard Menu. Fruit offers Hunter and Hunter offers Fruit, omitting their own mode link.

The playing field fills the entire outer board rectangle. Its rows and columns come from the available width and height, with a target cell size of 18–30 CSS pixels and bounds of 12–96 cells per axis. Fullscreen expands the playable area and increases its cell count. Terrain scales to each board while keeping adjacent wall cells connected. Live resize remaps the actors into legal, connected positions while preserving phase, elapsed time, score, boosts and learning. It is a recorded simulation action. The original 20-cell coordinates remain only as terrain templates and in the legacy replay engine. The sidebar places “A few handy moves” at its bottom on desktop; compact layouts keep setup and touch controls within one viewport.

Both opponents use genuine local neural behavior learning. Two small networks, each with 14 inputs, 12 tanh hidden units and four softmax outputs, train by backpropagation on successful human moves. Fruit movement in Escape/Challenges teaches the fruit policy; human-controlled snake movement in Hunter teaches the snake policy. The hunting snake predicts fruit turns and uses learned snake preferences in legal route selection. The evasive fruit combines learned fruit choices with a prediction of the player's snake movement. Easy, Normal and Hard use different anticipation thresholds, imitation strengths, commitment and movement cadences. Legal-move and route safeguards retain wall/body rules and the Wild Heart idle-capture regression. Learning is saved separately in `reverse-snake:learning:v1`, including on pause, completion and navigation. Failed storage retains the model for the current visit. More examples change predictions; this is behavior imitation, not a guarantee that every player's habits improve the opponent.

Before every match, players can choose Apple, Mango, Pear, Orange or Strawberry and Amber, Jade, Coral or Midnight snake designs. Apple and Jade are the fresh defaults. The fruit has distinct full artwork, and snake designs use different palettes and markings. Selections persist in `reverse-snake:appearance:v1`, freeze during active/paused matches, and are recorded for playback. Mango remains a selectable skin rather than the game's identity; Mango Grove is now Orchard Grove.

A matching Escape/Survival ghost draws the recorded full fruit artwork, composited once at 32% opacity. It has no collision or scoring effect. Matching includes the board geometry; a live resize clears an incompatible ghost. Pause uses a translucent blurred overlay while a separate cursor loops the most recent recorded gameplay, including both fruit and snake. If no earlier recording exists, it uses the current recorded prefix. The live round, its timers, model training and regular ghost cursor remain frozen. Reduced-motion preference keeps the preview still. Unscored preview storage does not add completed scores or replay-history entries.

New replay format 2 records initial board dimensions, cosmetics and neural weights, ordered inputs and resize events; deterministic training reproduces the evolving state and verifies the final fingerprint. Format 1 / version 1.1.0 / `canopy-2` recordings still play through the retained original engines. Three completed replays remain bounded to 250 KB each and 180,000 steps. Extra score entries omit neural weights to keep their storage bounded. Existing `reverse-snake:v1` scores remain readable, including abandoned-round semantics.

Local verification passes 54 rules tests, 53 browser tests with one existing headless visibility skip, lint, build, types and a zero-findings full audit. Verification is documented in [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md), visual captures in [VISUAL_DIRECTION.md](VISUAL_DIRECTION.md), and measured production behavior in [PERFORMANCE.md](PERFORMANCE.md). Coverage includes neural training and inference effects, persistence, tall/wide boards, resize without reset, old and new deterministic replays, ghost opacity, pause independence, all selectable artwork, native fullscreen, twelve main-game viewport sizes, themes, touch/key input and the original Wild Heart/Survival fixes.
